## Summary

- **Total**: 135 Pokemon
- **Starters**: 3 lines (Turtwig, Fuecoco, Totodile)

- **Paradox Pokemon**: Roaring Moon, Iron Valiant
- **Gift Pokemon**: Castform (Weather Institute)
- **Fossils**: Cover Fossil (Tirtouga) and Plume Fossil (Archen), revived at Devon Corp
